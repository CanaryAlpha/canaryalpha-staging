1. Who we are

Canary Alpha Research LLC is a Delaware limited liability company in the USA. We are responsible for the personal data described here. Privacy questions or requests: hello@canaryalpha.co. The Service is only for people 18 and older.

2. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Contact form: your name, email, phone number and an optional message.
  • Account signup: your account details, your confirmation that you are 18 or older, and the date and time you agreed to our Terms, Privacy Policy and Disclaimer.
  • Custom reports: the questions and details you share so we can prepare your report.

Collected automatically

  • Your theme choice (Light or Dark), saved in your browser’s local storage so the site remembers it.
  • Basic technical data our hosting provider records when you visit, such as IP address, browser type and pages requested, for security and to keep the site running.

From third parties

  • Stripe tells us whether a payment succeeded, along with basic billing details such as your name, email, country and the last four digits of your card. We never see or store your full card number.

3. How we use it

  • To provide the Service and your account.
  • To manage subscriptions, payments and refunds.
  • To prepare and deliver custom reports.
  • To reply to your messages.
  • To keep the Service secure and prevent spam and fraud.
  • To meet legal, accounting and tax duties.
  • To send service emails.

4. Marketing emails

We don’t currently send newsletters or marketing emails. We only send service emails, such as account, billing, security and report-related messages. If we start sending marketing emails, we will ask for your consent first, include an unsubscribe link in every email, and update this policy.

5. We don’t sell your data

We do not sell your personal data. We do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.

6. Who we share it with

We share data only with service providers that help us run the Service, and only for these purposes:

  • Stripe: payments and refunds. See Stripe’s Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.): hosts the website. Like any web host, it receives your IP address when you visit. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement.
  • Hostinger: hosts our email, including messages you send to hello@canaryalpha.co. See Hostinger’s Privacy Policy.
  • Formspree: receives what you send through our contact and join-the-list forms and forwards it to us by email. See Formspree’s Privacy Policy.
  • Google Fonts and Fontshare (Indian Type Foundry): serve the fonts our pages use. Your browser downloads the fonts from their servers, which receive your IP address. See Google’s Privacy Policy and Indian Type Foundry’s Privacy Policy.

We share data with legal authorities only when the law requires it.

7. Cookies and local storage

We use only essential storage:

  • Your theme choice, saved in your browser’s local storage.
  • Stripe’s own cookies during checkout, used to prevent fraud.

We don’t use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, so you won’t see a cookie banner. To clear what’s stored, use your browser’s settings to clear site data for our website.
