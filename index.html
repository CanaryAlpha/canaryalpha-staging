What makes Canary Alpha Research different?

We bring institutional-grade research to everyday investors. Our reports are easy to read, free to start, and built for retail investors who want depth without the jargon or the price tag.

How does the 80-point scoring framework work?

Each report pulls together multiple data points, from fundamentals to on-chain activity, and scores them using our proprietary 80-point framework. The result is a clear, consistent verdict you can compare across tokens.

What is whale-wallet forensics?

We analyze wallets holding at least 0.01% of a token’s supply and follow where their money moves. It shows you what the largest holders are doing, often before it shows up in the price.

Which tokens do you cover?

We research tokens ranked in the top 1,000 by market cap, and sometimes lower. We also use multiple sources to find promising tokens before they’re ranked at all.

How often are reports updated?

Reports are updated quarterly. Monthly updates are planned for the future.

Do you hold the tokens you research?

Sometimes. Canary Alpha Research and its founder may hold tokens we research. We disclose whether we hold a token, never how much, right after the report on it is published, on the report and on our Company Holdings page.
